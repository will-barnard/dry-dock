What these do
checkpoint
— the model, and the biggest lever here by far. Swap it before touching anything else.
workflow
— the recipe. txt2img is one pass; hires renders, upscales, then refines: better fine detail, roughly twice the time.
size
— 1024×1024 is SDXL's native resolution, not its "big" option. Smaller is genuinely worse, not just faster.
steps
— denoising iterations. 25–35 is the whole useful range; past ~50 you're burning time. With hires this drives the first pass only.
cfg
— how literally it obeys the prompt, not a quality dial. 5–7 is the band; higher goes contrasty and fried.
sampler
— the denoising algorithm. dpmpp_2m is the safe workhorse, dpmpp_3m_sde adds texture. Anything _ancestral never fully settles.
scheduler
— how noise is paced across the steps. karras is cleaner at moderate step counts; pair it with dpmpp_*.
seed
— the starting noise. Same seed and settings reproduce the same image exactly. Blank means random, and the one used is shown on the card.
batch
— several images in one job, sharing a seed. Cheaper than separate jobs because the model loads once.
start from an image
— img2img. Your picture is encoded and used as the starting point instead of pure noise. It's resized to about a megapixel first, and the output matches its shape.
how much to change it
— how much of your image to throw away. 0.2 retouches, 0.6 restyles, 0.85 keeps little but the composition, 1.0 ignores it entirely.
a starting point
— a photoreal checkpoint, sdxl_hires, 1024×1024, 30 steps, cfg 6, dpmpp_3m_sde + karras, seed blank. Then leave these alone and work on the prompt — it does more than any of them.
